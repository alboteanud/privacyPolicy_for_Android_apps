Privacy Policy

App: ElectriCode Pro
Package: com.turceni.electricode
Developer: Anghel Vasile
Last updated: October 2, 2026

In brief

ElectriCode Pro is an independent study tool for electrician licensing and certification exams. It has no user accounts or first-party analytics tools, and it does not send study data to the developer's servers. The free version displays ads; an optional PRO upgrade removes ads and unlocks additional features.

Data used by the app

The app may save the following locally in its device storage:

  • app preferences, including selected country, exam level, and topics;
  • practice answers, progress, and results;
  • favorite questions and questions saved in the personal mistake bank.

This study data is not sent to the developer. Android device backup settings may include app data in a device backup, subject to the user's settings and Android/Google policies.

Advertising and PRO upgrade

The free version includes ads provided through third-party advertising services. Those services may process device or advertising information to deliver and measure ads, according to their own privacy policies. Their data practices are governed by their respective policies.

The optional PRO upgrade is an in-app purchase. The app store where you download the app processes the purchase and manages payment information under its terms and privacy policy. The app uses purchase status to provide the PRO features.

Storage and deletion

Study preferences, practice answers, progress, results, favorites, and mistake-bank entries can be deleted by clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Copies already included in a device backup are managed separately by Android and the backup provider.

Educational information

ElectriCode Pro is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by any licensing authority or standards organization. Always verify information against the current official edition of the applicable electrical standards.
