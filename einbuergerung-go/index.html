Privacy Policy

App: Einbürgerung Go
Package: com.turceni.einbuergerunggo
Developer: Anghel Vasile
Last updated: November 6, 2026

In brief

Einbürgerung Go is an independent study app for the German citizenship test. It does not require an account. Study settings and progress are stored on your device. Optional AI explanations use Firebase AI services, optional question feedback can be sent by you, and optional PRO purchases are handled by Google Play. The app does not include advertising or analytics services in its current implementation.

Information stored on your device

The app stores study and app data locally, which may include your selected Bundesland and study scope, language preference, favorites, questions in your mistake bank and review streaks, daily usage limits, and an active quiz or exam session with its answers and timing information. Purchase status may also be stored locally to enable PRO features. The app does not synchronize this study state to a developer-operated account; the optional transmissions described below happen only when you use AI explanations or send feedback.

Android backup and device transfer are enabled for app data, except downloaded official documents. Other local app data may be included in a backup depending on your device and backup settings. Android and the backup provider manage those copies under their own settings and policies.

Optional AI explanations

If you request an AI explanation and the feature is configured in your app version, the app sends the selected question and its study context to Firebase AI Logic for processing with Google's Gemini service. The request can include the question text, answer choices and answer, existing study explanation and source context, selected Bundesland, and requested language. Firebase App Check is also used with this feature. Google and Firebase process this information under their own terms and privacy policies. AI explanations are optional; you can study without using them.

Question feedback

If you choose to report a question issue or suggestion, the feedback includes the selected Bundesland and catalog information, question identifier and text, and the message you enter. In app versions configured with a feedback endpoint, this information is sent over the internet to a Google Apps Script web app and may be saved in its associated spreadsheet for review. Google may process it under its own policies.

If no feedback endpoint is configured, the app opens an email composer with the report information. The app does not send the email; you choose the recipient and whether to send it. Your email provider's privacy practices apply.

Google Play purchases

Optional weekly or annual subscriptions and a lifetime PRO purchase are processed by Google Play through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment and purchase information under its own terms and privacy policy. The app receives purchase status from Google Play to provide or restore PRO features; it does not send purchase information to a developer-operated server.

Official documents and external links

The app can download official study documents directly from the German Federal Office for Migration and Refugees (BAMF) and keep them in private app storage for offline use. Downloaded documents are excluded from Android backup and device transfer. Opening a source link or downloading a document connects your device to the linked publisher; that publisher may receive standard connection information under its own privacy policy.

Einbürgerung Go is not affiliated with or endorsed by BAMF. The app does not include advertising or a dedicated analytics SDK in its current implementation. It requests internet access for its optional services, document downloads, and Google Play Billing; it does not request location, contacts, microphone, or SMS permissions.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored app data can be deleted by clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Backup copies, if any, are managed separately by Android and your backup provider. Feedback already sent by email or through a configured endpoint is handled by the relevant email or Google service and may remain there; contact the developer with privacy questions or requests. Information sent to Google for AI explanations is handled under Google's applicable terms and policies.
