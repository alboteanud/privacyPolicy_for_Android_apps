Privacy Policy

App: Photo Privacy Cleaner
Android package: com.turceni.photoprivacycleaner
Last updated: November 12, 2026

In brief

Photo Privacy Cleaner processes selected photos on your device. It does not upload photos, photo metadata, or editing activity to the developers. The app has no developer account, advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting service. Google Play Billing is used for the optional one-time PRO purchase and requires communication with Google Play.

Photos and metadata

You choose an image using Android's system photo picker or share an image to the app from another app. The selected image is decoded and edited in memory. The app does not copy the original image into its own files, cache, or preferences. The original remains unchanged.

The app creates a new, flattened PNG for your clean copy. Hidden metadata, including GPS location, dates, camera details, comments, and embedded image metadata, is removed by default. You may choose specific supported metadata fields to keep or edit; only those selected fields are added to the exported image. Redacting pixels does not remove private information that remains visible in the image. Blur is not a guarantee of anonymity, so inspect the final preview before exporting.

Editing state is held temporarily in memory and is lost if the app process ends. Clearing the session or closing the app drops its references to the image, but this is not a promise of forensic erasure from device memory.

Saving and sharing

When you choose Save, Android's system document creator lets you select where to write the reviewed PNG. The app writes only the clean output to that destination. A chosen cloud or third-party file provider may upload, retain, or otherwise process the file under its own terms and privacy policy.

When you choose Share, Android opens the share sheet and the app provides the clean output through a temporary, read-only in-memory link. The link is available only for the most recently shared output and expires after 15 minutes, when you edit, replace, or clear the image, when the activity finishes, or when the app process ends. A recipient may keep a copy or an already-open stream; the app cannot retract those copies. The receiving app handles the image under its own privacy practices.

The system photo picker or a selected cloud provider may download or transfer a photo when you choose it. Such transfers are handled by Android or that provider, not by a developer server.

Local app data and purchases

The app stores the current local calendar day's export count and a cached PRO purchase entitlement in private app preferences. Photos and photo metadata are not stored there. Free use allows three saves and shares combined per day; the count resets at local midnight. The app does not use this information for advertising or profiling.

PRO is a one-time, non-consumable purchase through Google Play, not a subscription. Google Play processes purchase and payment information under Google's terms and privacy policy. The app checks and restores purchase status with Google Play; it does not receive or store your payment card details. No photo content is sent to Google Play Billing. Purchase verification and entitlement storage are client-side, with no developer-operated purchase server.

Permissions and security

The app does not request broad photo-library, storage, or camera permission. You grant access to an image and save destination through Android's system interfaces. Network and billing access are used for Google Play purchases. App data is excluded from Android backup and device transfer, and screenshots and recent-app previews are blocked by the app. These measures cannot guarantee security on a compromised device or control copies made outside the app.

Retention and deletion

Images are kept only in memory while you use the app unless you explicitly save or share them. Local export-count and cached purchase-status preferences remain until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the app's local preferences. It does not delete images you saved, copies made by receiving apps, or files retained by a selected provider. Manage those copies with the relevant app or provider.

Children

Photo Privacy Cleaner is a general-purpose utility and does not provide developer-operated accounts or collect personal information from children through its own servers.
