Delete Your Stillwatch Account

App: Stillwatch
Package: com.turceni.remotesurveillance
Developer: Anghel Vasile
Last updated: November 4, 2026

Request account and data deletion

If you can sign in to Stillwatch:

  1. Sign in, open the account menu, and choose Delete account.
  2. Type DELETE to confirm. Reauthenticate with your password or Google account when prompted. Account deletion removes your Stillwatch Firebase Authentication account and its associated Firebase Realtime Database records.

If you cannot access your account or use the in-app option, send an email from the email address associated with your Stillwatch account to vasile.anghel.1957@gmail.com or alboteanud@gmail.com. Use the subject Stillwatch account deletion and state that you want your account and associated cloud data deleted. If you signed in with Google, use the email address associated with that account. We may ask you to verify ownership; never send your password or sign-in codes. Our target for email requests is to complete deletion within 30 days after verifying account ownership. We will confirm completion by email or explain any delay.

Before submitting a deletion request, stop camera standby on your phones if possible. Do not uninstall the app as a substitute for deleting your account.

Data deleted

Account deletion removes your Firebase Authentication account and its associated records from Firebase Realtime Database. This includes:

  • Your Stillwatch Firebase Authentication account and its associated sign-in profile.
  • Camera registrations, including device identifiers, names, online status, and last-seen timestamps.
  • Viewing-session records, including camera and viewer identifiers, session times, status, and viewer presence records.
  • Any remaining surveillance snapshots stored with those sessions.

Deletion is permanent. It removes access to the Stillwatch account on all connected phones. Deleting your Stillwatch account does not delete your Google account. Signing in again after deletion may create a new Stillwatch account.

Data retained or managed separately

There is no additional routine retention period for the account, camera, session, or snapshot records in Firebase Authentication and the app's active Firebase Realtime Database after deletion is completed. Deletion-request correspondence is used to verify ownership, process the request, and send confirmation; it is not intended as a permanent account record.

Google may retain service logs or backup copies under its own retention practices; these are not governed by the app's session expiry or the completion target above. See Firebase Privacy and Security and the Google data retention policy. If specific records must be retained to meet a legal obligation, we will explain the applicable reason and retention period when responding to your request, unless prohibited by law.

Images saved to Pictures/Stillwatch on any phone are not remotely deleted. Delete them using that phone's gallery or media app, including its trash or recently deleted folder. Delete any cloud-gallery backups separately through the relevant provider. These copies remain until you or the provider removes them. Clearing local app data or uninstalling the app does not replace an account-deletion request and may not remove shared gallery images.
