Privacy Policy

App: PageVoice
Android package: com.turceni.pdftovoice
Last updated: November 11, 2026

In brief

PageVoice reads PDF, TXT, Markdown, and CSV documents aloud. Documents you select are processed on your device. The app does not operate user accounts or its own document servers, and its current implementation does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. Internet access is used for HTTPS document downloads and Google Play Billing.

Documents and permissions

You choose local documents through Android's system document picker. PageVoice accesses only the document you select and does not request broad storage access. Android may let the app retain access to a selected document so it can appear in Recent reads and be reopened later. You can manage the document and its access through Android or its file provider.

The app extracts document text on-device. PDFs with no extractable text may be processed with on-device text recognition. During processing, the selected document is copied to a temporary cache file, which the app deletes after extraction, including when processing fails. Extracted text is held in memory while the document is open and is not saved as a document copy by the app. Text files are read as plain text; Markdown and CSV formatting characters may remain.

Documents are limited to 50 MB, 2,000 PDF pages, and two million extracted characters. The app currently cannot unlock password-protected PDFs. Complex layouts may affect reading order.

HTTPS document links

If you enter an HTTPS link, PageVoice downloads the linked document to open it. This feature requires PageVoice Pro. The linked website receives the request and may process your IP address, the requested URL, and other connection information under its own privacy practices. Avoid links containing secrets or personal information. Downloads use a temporary cache file that the app deletes after extraction; the app does not upload documents to a developer-operated server.

Speech and installed voice services

PageVoice uses Android's text-to-speech service and selects an installed voice that does not require a network connection. Document text is passed to the selected speech engine on the device for playback. The app does not bundle voice models or automatically download them. If you choose to install voice data, Android opens the speech engine's installer; the engine or its provider may process that choice under its own terms and privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

PageVoice stores language and playback-speed preferences, your local Pro entitlement, and up to 10 recent document titles and source references (such as a selected document URI or an HTTPS link) so you can reopen documents. It does not save extracted document text as reading history. A selected document may no longer be available if it is moved, deleted, or its access is revoked.

Android backup and device-transfer behavior depends on your device and settings. The app's current backup rules exclude its local billing preference but do not exclude its other app preferences, which may include reading history, language, and speed. Android system behavior and provider policies apply to any backup or transfer.

Purchases and third-party services

PageVoice offers an optional one-time PageVoice Pro purchase through Google Play Billing; it is not a subscription. Google Play processes purchase and payment information under Google's terms and privacy policy. PageVoice receives purchase status and stores a local Pro entitlement to unlock features. The app does not receive or store your full payment details.

The app includes Google Play Billing and on-device text recognition libraries. It does not include a dedicated advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK. Android, Google Play, speech engines, file providers, and websites you choose may process information under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Recent reads and preferences remain in app storage until changed, cleared, or removed with the app's data. Uninstalling PageVoice or clearing its storage removes local app data, subject to Android backup and device-transfer behavior. Uninstalling does not delete your original documents, files held by another provider, or any device backups. Temporary cache files are deleted by the app after extraction; Android may clear app cache independently.

Children

PageVoice is a general-purpose document reader and is not specifically directed at children. The developers do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service.
