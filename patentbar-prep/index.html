Privacy Policy

App: PatentBar Prep
Package: com.turceni.patentbarprep
Developer: Anghel Vasile
Last updated: November 6, 2026

In brief

PatentBar Prep is an independent study app for the U.S. Patent Bar registration examination. It does not require an account and does not include advertising or developer-operated analytics. Study settings and progress are stored on your device. Some optional features contact Google services, Google Play, or official source websites as described below.

Information stored on your device

The app stores information needed for its study features, which may include your selected exam and study track, favorites, questions marked for review, study and explanation usage counts, an active session and its answers, language preferences, and the local status of your PRO purchase. Official PDFs that you download for offline reading and related download metadata are also stored on your device.

This information is kept in the app's private storage and is not routinely sent to the developer. Android backup or device transfer may include some app data depending on your device, Android version, and settings. The app excludes its downloaded official-document folder from Android cloud backup. Copies managed by Android or a backup provider are subject to that provider's policies.

AI explanations

If AI explanations are configured in your version, you can choose to request one for a study question. The app sends Google, through Firebase AI Logic, the question text and answer choices, the study-bank correct answer, and related study material such as the explanation, references, source link, exam context, language, and review status. It does not send your selected response, saved progress, favorites, or study history as part of this request. Google processes the request and generated explanation under the applicable Google and Firebase terms and privacy policies. See Firebase Privacy and Security and Google's Privacy Policy.

In release builds, Firebase App Check may also use Google Play Integrity to help verify requests from the app. AI explanations are unavailable in builds without the required service configuration. The app applies a daily request limit, stored locally on your device.

Feedback

If you report a question problem, the report includes the app name, exam or study context, question identifier and text, and the feedback you enter. Depending on how the app version is configured, it either sends this information over HTTPS to a Google Apps Script service, where it is recorded in the associated Google Sheet, or opens an email draft for you to review and send with an email app. In the email option, the app does not send anything until you choose to send it. Do not include sensitive personal information in a report.

Feedback sent through the configured service is retained in the associated spreadsheet until it is deleted by its owner. Google may process connection information when the service receives a report. For a privacy question or a request about a report, contact the developer below.

Purchases

Optional weekly or annual subscriptions and a one-time PRO purchase are processed by Google Play. Google Play handles payment details under its own terms and privacy policy; the app does not receive your full payment-card details. The app checks purchase status with Google Play and stores the resulting PRO access status on your device so features can work between checks. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play.

Official websites and documents

When you open an official source or download an offline document, your device connects directly to the relevant website, such as the USPTO or another cited source. Those sites may receive your IP address and standard request information and are governed by their own privacy policies. The app does not route these requests through a developer-operated server. Downloaded PDFs are kept locally until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Retention and deletion

Local study information and downloaded documents remain on your device until removed in the app, where an in-app removal option is available, or until you clear PatentBar Prep's app data or uninstall the app. Backup copies, feedback already sent, purchase records held by Google Play, and information received by Google or official websites are managed separately under the relevant provider's policies. To ask about deleting feedback, contact the developer. There is no account to delete.

Permissions and children's privacy

The app uses internet access for optional online features and Google Play billing. It does not request location, contacts, microphone, or SMS permissions. PatentBar Prep is a general study tool and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer at vasile.anghel.1957@gmail.com or alboteanud@gmail.com.
