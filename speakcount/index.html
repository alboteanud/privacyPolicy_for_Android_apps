Privacy Policy

App: SpeakCount
Android package: com.turceni.wordscounter
Developer: Anghel Vasile
Last updated: October 30, 2026

In brief

SpeakCount counts spoken words on your device and can attribute word counts to detected or enrolled speakers. Speech recognition and speaker matching run locally on your phone using offline Vosk models. The app explicitly removes the Android INTERNET permission, does not send audio, transcripts, voice profiles, or word counts to developer servers, and does not save audio recordings or spoken text transcripts. Optional language model downloads and optional Premium purchases are handled on your device through Google Play.

Microphone and on-device audio processing

When you start listening or record a voice sample to enroll a person, SpeakCount captures microphone audio using an Android foreground microphone service. Audio is processed in memory at 16 kHz mono PCM by the on-device speech and speaker recognition models.

  • No audio recordings stored: raw audio is analyzed in memory and discarded immediately after processing.
  • No transcripts stored: when "Show live text" is enabled, the currently heard words are displayed on screen in memory only and are never saved to storage.
  • Consent of people nearby: because the microphone can pick up nearby voices while listening is active, you are responsible for informing and obtaining any required consent from people around you before starting a listening session.

Data stored on your device

SpeakCount stores the following information in app-private storage on your device (in a local Room database and SharedPreferences) to provide its features:

  • Word counts and listening time: session word totals, daily word totals by date, per-speaker word counts, and session listening duration;
  • Speaker profiles (voice embeddings): speaker names or labels (such as "Person 1" or names you enter), numeric voiceprint vectors (x-vector embeddings) derived from speech, and frame counts used to match utterances to speakers on your device;
  • App settings and entitlement state: listening preferences (such as skip silence, live text display, auto-stop timer, automatic speaker detection, voice match strictness, and selected speech language) and cached Premium purchase status.

When automatic speaker detection is enabled, the app creates local speaker profiles for new voices and updates automatic voiceprint vectors as additional speech is matched. Enrolled speaker profiles created by recording a voice sample are stored locally in the same database. Voice embeddings are mathematical representations used only for local speaker attribution in the app and should not be used for identity verification or authentication.

Permissions and system integration

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): required to hear speech, count words, and enroll or match speakers. Listening starts only when initiated by you in the visible app.
  • Foreground service and wake lock (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE, WAKE_LOCK): used to keep counting active while the screen is locked or another app is open, accompanied by an ongoing notification with a Stop action.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used to display the active listening status and current session word count in the system notification area.
  • No Internet permission: the app removes android.permission.INTERNET from its manifest and cannot make direct network connections.

If you add the home-screen widget or Quick Settings tile, Android displays your current session and daily word counts on that surface.

Google Play Billing and language downloads

SpeakCount offers optional Premium subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase through Google Play Billing, as well as optional downloadable speech recognition models for additional languages through Google Play Asset Delivery.

Because SpeakCount itself has no internet access, billing requests and on-demand language pack downloads are carried out through the Google Play Store app on your device. Google Play processes payment details, subscription management, and asset downloads under Google's own terms and privacy policy. SpeakCount receives only the localized offer details, purchase entitlement status, and installed model files from Google Play on the device.

Backups, retention, and deletion

Android cloud backup and device-to-device data extraction are disabled in the app's configuration, and the app's database, preferences, and unpacked speech models are excluded from automatic Android backup.

Word counts, speaker profiles, and settings remain on your device until you remove them:

  • You can reset the current session count at any time in the app.
  • You can rename, merge, re-record, or remove individual speaker profiles in the People section; removing a person deletes their voice profile and per-person word records.
  • You can remove downloaded language packs in the app's Speech language settings.
  • Clearing SpeakCount's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored word counts, history, speaker voice profiles, and settings.

Advertising, analytics, and third-party sharing

SpeakCount does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs, and does not sell or share your personal data or voice profiles with third parties.

Children's privacy

SpeakCount is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. The developer does not collect personal information from children or operate any server that receives user data from the app.
